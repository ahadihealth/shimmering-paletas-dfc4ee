Our Story

One promise, kept two ways

"Ahadi" is Swahili for promise. It started as our promise to residents in long term care who needed wound healing brought to them. It now extends to a promise for patients and families who want a primary care relationship built on trust, not turnover.

Compassionate

Every patient's journey is treated as its own, not a protocol to move through.

Evidence Based

Meet Our Founder

My Journey

My journey in healthcare began in 2006 as a nursing aide. From the very beginning, I knew I wanted a career centered on serving others. Caring for patients gave me a sense of purpose, but it also showed me that I wanted to do more.

I became a Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) and worked in long term care, where I developed a deep appreciation for caring for older adults. As my experience grew, I often encountered situations where I wished I had the knowledge and authority to do more for my patients. That desire inspired me to continue my education.

I earned my certification as a Medication Technician (CMT/CMA) before pursuing my nursing career. I became a Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN), then earned my Associate Degree in Nursing to become a Registered Nurse (RN) in 2009. I later completed my Bachelor of Science in Nursing in 2014. My passion for patient care continued to grow, leading me to earn a Master of Science in Nursing and become a board certified Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP-C). I am also certified in Advanced Wound Care.

Throughout my career, one thing has never changed: my commitment to my patients. Whether that means traveling to see someone in their home, providing specialized wound care, or creating new ways to make primary care more accessible, I believe patients deserve compassionate, high quality care wherever they are.

My clinical experience has been built one step at a time, serving patients in hospitals, long term care facilities, skilled nursing facilities, home health, and community based settings. Every role has strengthened my belief that healthcare should be personal, accessible, and centered around the individual, not the system.

Outside of work, I am married to a beautiful wife, Maureen, and we have been blessed with two beautiful girls. I love spending time with family; we love traveling and exploring. I love music, and I play drums part time for a local church.

Why Ahadi Health?

After spending more than a decade working within large healthcare systems as both a Registered Nurse and a Family Nurse Practitioner, I saw firsthand how difficult it can be for patients to receive timely, personalized care.

Many patients face obstacles such as insurance restrictions, high deductibles, long wait times, transportation challenges, and limited appointment availability. These barriers often prevent people from getting the care they need when they need it most.

I founded Ahadi Health with a simple mission: make healthcare more accessible, more personal, and more convenient.

Our goal is to bring healthcare closer to our patients by offering mobile services, providing longer and more meaningful visits, and giving patients healthcare options that are not limited by traditional insurance models.

Mobile Wound Care

Our mobile wound care program serves patients throughout the Kansas City area by bringing specialized wound care directly to their homes, assisted living communities, and skilled nursing facilities.

While we accept insurance for wound care services, our mission goes beyond treating wounds. We are continually looking for innovative ways to make advanced wound care more accessible so that patients receive expert treatment without unnecessary barriers or travel.

Direct Primary Care

We believe primary care should be simple, affordable, and focused entirely on the patient. Our Direct Primary Care membership is designed for individuals without insurance, those with high deductible health plans, small business employees, and anyone who wants a more personal relationship with their healthcare provider.

For one affordable monthly membership fee, patients receive convenient access to comprehensive primary care without the stress of insurance driven medicine.

At Ahadi Health, our commitment is straightforward: deliver compassionate, accessible healthcare that puts patients first. We are constantly looking for better ways to serve our community and ensure that quality healthcare remains within reach for everyone.

Privacy & Security

How we handle your information

We only ask for what we need to arrange your care, and every form on this site requires your consent before submitting. Facility referrals intentionally exclude resident names and birthdates until a referral is confirmed.

Note for the site owner: this page is a static front end demo. Collecting health information (PHI) in a way that's actually HIPAA compliant is an infrastructure and process commitment, not just page copy: it requires a secure backend, encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and audit logs, signed Business Associate Agreements with any hosting or form vendor, staff training, and a real Notice of Privacy Practices reviewed by counsel. None of that exists in this mockup yet; treat every "submitted" form here as a design preview only.
